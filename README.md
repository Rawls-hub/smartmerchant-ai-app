SmartMerchant AI

Intelligent Business Management & Retail Intelligence

Built by Rawls & Hub | Everything Techy

SmartMerchant AI is an AI-powered business management platform designed to help small businesses and retailers manage their daily operations, understand their financial performance, and make smarter business decisions.

The platform combines business management tools with AI-driven insights to simplify retail operations, improve inventory management, track payments, and provide actionable business intelligence.

Project Overview

Small businesses often struggle with disconnected business records, inventory tracking, financial management, and understanding their actual profitability.

SmartMerchant AI addresses these challenges through a unified platform that brings essential business operations together and makes business data easier to understand and act upon.

Key Features

1. Sales & Point of Sale (POS)

* Record and manage sales transactions.
* Track sales performance.
* Monitor transaction records.

2. Inventory Management

* Manage product inventory.
* Monitor stock levels.
* Track product movement and sales velocity.
* Identify products requiring restocking.

3. Order Management

* Create and manage customer orders.
* Track order progress and records.

4. Customer Management

* Maintain customer records.
* Organise customer information.
* Support better customer relationship management.

5. Payments & Debts

* Track paid, unpaid, and partially paid transactions.
* Calculate outstanding balances.
* Monitor customer debts and payment records.

6. Expense Management

* Record and categorise business expenses.
* Monitor operational spending.
* Support financial performance analysis.

7. AI Business Insights

* Analyse business performance.
* Understand revenue, expenses, and profit.
* Identify business trends and opportunities.
* Support data-informed business decisions.

8. Ask SmartMerchant AI

An AI-powered business assistant that answers natural-language questions using available business records.

It is designed to:

* Answer questions about sales and inventory.
* Analyse expenses and profitability.
* Provide insights into customer payments and outstanding debts.
* Explain business performance over selected periods.
* Use actual business records rather than inventing financial figures.
